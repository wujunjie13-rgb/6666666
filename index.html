Latin Pronunciation Guide

Basic Rules

  • Vowels: Always pronounced clearly
    • a = "ah" (as in "father")
    • e = "eh" (as in "bed")
    • i = "ee" (as in "machine")
    • o = "oh" (as in "note")
    • u = "oo" (as in "rude")
  • c: Always hard, like "k" (cervical = KER-vih-kal)
  • g: Always hard, like in "gate"
  • ae: Pronounced as "eye" or "eh"
  • ph: Pronounced as "f"
  • ch: Pronounced as "k"
